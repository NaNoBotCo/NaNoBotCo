# Security policy

Reports of a security fault are welcome across NaNoBotCo's projects, and for the
map directory at **motdang.net** they are paid.

## Bug bounty — motdang.net

A previously-unknown, reproducible security bug in motdang.net or its API is
worth **฿1,000 (about US$30)**, and more when the bug is serious — our call.
Every accepted report is credited by name, or kept anonymous if you prefer.

Someone who shows us a fault is showing us where the treasure is buried.

- Full scope, reward and rules: https://motdang.net/security
- Machine-readable (RFC 9116): https://motdang.net/.well-known/security.txt

## How to report

- LINE: [@964yxgnk](https://line.me/R/ti/p/~@964yxgnk)
- Form: https://motdang.net/mail

Give the steps to reproduce and what happens. Report privately first, and give
us time to fix before you publish. Good-faith testing within the scope at the
link above is welcome and authorised — do not reach, change or delete data that
is not yours, and stop as soon as you meet personal data.

## Other repositories

For a security issue in another NaNoBotCo repository, open a private security
advisory on that repository, or reach the contacts above.
